આપણું પંચાંગ (Aapnu Panchang) – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 September 2026

Aapnu Panchang is a Gujarati calendar and panchang app. All panchang, choghadiya, festival and muhurat calculations are done on your device. The app does not require an account and does not connect to any server of ours.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

Location

If you tap the “GPS” button, the app asks for approximate (coarse) location permission to calculate sunrise, sunset and choghadiya for your place. The location is used only on your phone, saved only in the app's local settings, and is never sent anywhere. You can instead choose a city manually, and you can revoke the permission at any time in Android settings.

Notifications

With your permission, the app shows local reminders (daily panchang, vrat, festival and choghadiya alerts). These are generated on the device; no data leaves the phone.

Data stored on your device

Your preferences (city or GPS coordinates, language, font size, theme, notification choices) are stored locally and are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
